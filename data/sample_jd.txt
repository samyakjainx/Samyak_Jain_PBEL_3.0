Job Title: Data Scientist / Machine Learning Engineer

Company: TechNova Solutions

Location: Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)

Job Summary:
We are looking for a skilled Data Scientist / Machine Learning Engineer to join our
growing AI team. The ideal candidate will design, build, and deploy machine learning
models that solve real business problems, working closely with product and engineering
teams.

Key Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets to extract actionable insights
- Build, train, and evaluate machine learning and deep learning models
- Develop and maintain data pipelines for model training and inference
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deploy models into production
- Communicate findings clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Stay current with the latest research in machine learning and NLP

Required Skills:
- Strong programming skills in Python
- Experience with machine learning frameworks such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch
- Solid understanding of statistics, data analysis, and feature engineering
- Experience with natural language processing (NLP) techniques
- Proficiency with SQL and relational databases
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure
- Experience with data visualization tools (e.g. Power BI, Tableau)
- Version control experience with Git and GitHub
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills
- Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with deep learning and large language models (LLMs)
- Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes for model deployment
- Prior experience in a data science or analytics role
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field

What We Offer:
- Competitive salary and benefits
- Flexible hybrid work environment
- Opportunities for continuous learning and career growth
- Collaborative and inclusive team culture
